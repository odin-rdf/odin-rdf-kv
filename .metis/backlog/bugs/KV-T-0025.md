## Objective

Make `test_del_reader_keeps_snapshot` (`tests/delete_test.odin`, written in KV-T-0018 for KV-I-0003 NFR-003) pass for every seed, by asserting what NFR-003 needs rather than a stricter condition the store does not promise.

## Status Updates

### 2026-09-24

**Cause: the assertion was too strict; the store is right.** The test held a reader across 20 rounds of random deletes and puts and asserted `free_ready == 0` at the end. But a page a write transaction allocates and drops again in the same transaction (a key put and then deleted in one round, or an overflow run replaced) is *loose* (`Write_State.loose`, KV-I-0003 D8), and `freelist_build` lists leftover loose pages as reusable at once: no reader ever saw them. Instrumenting `freelist_build` and the test for the seed above showed it: commit S=5 left loose pages 869 and 870 (ready after round 4, taken again in round 5), and commit S=21 left page 1376, which was the `free_ready` of 1. The reader's snapshot ends at page 685, so every one of these pages lies past it. Whether the final round leaves a loose page depends on the seed. The instrumentation is removed.

**Fix.** The `free_ready == 0` assertion is replaced by a check after every round: no page in `env.free.ready` is one the reader's snapshot can see. A new file-private helper, `snapshot_has_page`, counts a page as seen if it lies in `[2, last_pgno]` of the snapshot and isn't one of the free pages its free-list records (read from the map); the run itself counts as seen, since `space_check` on the reader reads it. The rest of the test is unchanged. Checking every round rather than only at the end is stronger: a reader's page listed as reusable in any round would be caught, even if the next round took it again.

**Seeds tried:** 778714601451138 and 1 to 19, all passing.

**Deliberate-bug check:** `reuse_horizon` in `kv/txn.odin` changed to ignore the oldest reader. The fixed test failed at round 2 ("page 50 of the reader's snapshot is reusable") and in every round after; restored.

**Verification:** `scripts/test.sh` (debug, `-o:speed`, `-sanitize:address`, and the four `odin check` targets): 132 tests, all passing. Steady-state tests not run: they don't touch this test.

Note: KV-T-0018's summary of this test ("Nothing is reusable while it's held") described the old assertion; the accurate claim is that nothing the reader can see is reusable while it's held.
