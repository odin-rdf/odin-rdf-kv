# Delete verification: model, readers, churn and fill measurement

## Parent Initiative

[[KV-I-0003]]

## Objective

Show that `del` holds up under randomized and concurrent use and in the steady state (KV-I-0003 NFR-003, NFR-004, D1), measure how full trees are after deletes, and close the initiative.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The randomized model (`tests/model.odin`, `run_model`) includes `del` of present and absent keys against the oracle, with `space_check` after every commit and reopen, held readers checked against their snapshots, and full scans in both directions.
- [ ] NFR-003: a reader holding a snapshot across a commit that deletes half the keys (some with overflow values) still reads every one of them, then the pages are reused after it ends.
- [ ] The threaded isolation test includes deletes and passes under `-sanitize:thread` on macOS arm64, run more than once.
- [ ] NFR-004: a churn steady state (inserts and deletes over a moving key set, no readers) keeps `last_pgno` bounded, on demand with the other steady tests (`KV_STEADY`).
- [ ] Deleting every key and committing gives the empty tree, with every page but the meta pages on the free list or its run (`space_check`), and the file reused by a following reload.
- [ ] Fill measurement: average and minimum leaf and branch fill after deleting 50% and 90% of keys at random, against an insert-only tree. Reported in the Results, not asserted.
- [ ] `scripts/test.sh`, `scripts/test.sh --steady` once, and `scripts/test-linux.sh` on arm64 and amd64 pass.
- [ ] The vision's Current State, `CLAUDE.md` (next up, code map) and the initiative's Results section (exit criteria table, deviations, known limitations) are updated.

## Implementation Notes

### Technical Approach
- Weight the model's operation mix so the tree both grows and shrinks, including phases that empty it, so root collapse and regrowth from `root = 0` are exercised.
- The churn workload should delete keys that span overflow values and different key sizes, so merges happen on leaves and branches.

### Dependencies
KV-T-0017.

### Risk Considerations
- A low fill after random deletes is the known cost of D1 (no borrowing). If it is severe, record it and file borrowing as a backlog item rather than changing the design here.
